# Local PDF research

The user-provided `pdfs/` folder is inventoried in `catalog/research/local-pdf-inventory.json` with SHA-256, page counts and research cache paths. Original PDFs, raster pages and extracted book text remain outside catalog archives.

Gazetteers are identified from their contents rather than filename edition labels. The Atruaghin guide pair credited to Glen Welch is a fan revision, not original GAZ14. Original TSR books and later fan supplements remain distinct sources.

Image-only Five Shires and Ylaruam scans have OCR search caches. OCR is a page-finding aid; selected item names and values are checked visually before verification. OCR text is not itself a completed book audit. Remaining image-only books require further work.

Existing item records gain checks through `catalog/settings/mystara/local-pdf-verifications.json`, preserving their earlier secondary evidence. New original-page facts appear in the separate local-PDF reference pack. Every check lists PDF page, printed page, file hash and its limited verification scope.

The first pass verifies selected Karameikos NPC equipment, Ylaruam glossary objects, Ierendi export goods, Blackflame crafting costs, Minrothad relic crafts and Darokin NPC equipment. Legacy construction costs are not retail prices; the polished Ierendi fire-starter range remains a legacy market fact and does not automatically set a 2024 Charisma-adjusted quote.

The original Ylaruam glossary identifies a Yataghan as a scimitar. Its project reskin now uses the SRD 2024 Scimitar profile instead of the earlier Shortsword mapping, with the change retained in its conversion notes.
