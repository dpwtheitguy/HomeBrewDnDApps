# Spell scroll migration to D&D 2024

Keep the original 3.5 spell name, scroll tradition, source spell level, price, and page or table locator as provenance. Resolve a modern item by its verified 2024 spell and the level recorded on the modern scroll. Arcane and divine source rows can point to the same modern scroll when both resolve to the same spell and level. Record renamed or replacement spells explicitly. Leave uncertain mappings and legacy-only spells available as historical references until reviewed.

For the published Spell Scroll, check whether the spell is on the character’s spell list. If it is above the level the character can normally cast, use a spellcasting ability check with DC 10 plus the scroll spell level. Use the spell’s normal casting time. Published scroll rarity, save DC, and spell attack bonus come from the modern scroll level. Track player-scribed scrolls separately because their rules use the scribe’s save DC and attack bonus. Preserve scribing cost and historical price separately from any modern buy or sell estimate.

Fighter eligibility should come from the actual character’s spell list, including applicable subclass, multiclass, or other features. Keep the inventory and trading actions available independently of casting eligibility.
