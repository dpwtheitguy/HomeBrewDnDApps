# Mystara games and novels

Queued after the current Gazetteer pass: Shadow over Mystara (also searchable as “Shadow of Mystara”), Tower of Doom, Order of the Griffon, Warriors of the Eternal Sun, Fantasy Empires, D&D Stronghold (1993), and Chronicles of Mystara. Stronghold's precise setting placement needs verification; it is distinct from Stronghold Crusader. Chronicles is a compilation and must not duplicate the two arcade inventories.

The novel queue enumerates the Penhaligon Trilogy, Dragonlord Chronicles, Rogues to Riches, Son of Dawn, Dark Knight of Karameikos and The Black Vessel. This is an initial bibliography, not a claim that every Mystara-related novel or gamebook has been found.

No equipment was inferred from a title or cover. Item imports require passage-level or attributed item-specific evidence. Future records must retain game/novel origin, source references, adaptation changes and verification status. No novel prose or game art is distributed by this registry.

Sources: https://pandius.com/prodlist.html and https://pandius.com/vgamtime.html .
