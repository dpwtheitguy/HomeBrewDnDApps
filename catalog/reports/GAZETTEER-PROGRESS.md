## Remaining limits

- Publisher previews are partial, including map panels and introductory pages.
- Names and appearances were checked on selected pages; rules and modern compatibility were not.
- NPC inventories are secondary reports, not verified original NPC stat blocks.
- Trade categories are not individual item variants; no price table or tax formula imported.
- GAZ7 construction/furnishings are not additional portable magic items.
- No 24-rune item multiplication: rune symbols are not 24 physical equipment records.

The Gazetteer pass examines mundane, cultural, military, trade and construction material as well as magic items. Regional presence is tagged found-in rather than treated as proof of local manufacture. Identical NPC equipment labels are grouped within a book with all reported appearances preserved.
