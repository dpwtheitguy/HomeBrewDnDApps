## Cosmetic premiums

| Price adjustment | Records |
|---|---:|
| 3.5e masterwork price benchmark | 84 |
| Decorative workmanship · +20% | 78 |
| Jewelled ornament · +50% | 6 |

Published and already recorded item prices are preserved. Percentage premiums apply to the physical equipment value. The historical masterwork benchmark affects price only in this appraisal.

Original prices and currencies remain in each record’s provenance. The price supplement supplies a market reference with its own economy, basis, and unit scope. Category and cross-edition comparisons are estimates.

Neutral merchandise quotes use +0 Persuasion, neutral attitude, and Balanced pricing. Explicit source resale amounts are retained as sell benchmarks. Construction quotes are independent appraisals. Coin display rounds to copper with a one-copper minimum. Story items, artifacts, and fixed installations have indicative numbers for DM use.
